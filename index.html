The Company launches its annual sustainability campaign.

The Company today launched its annual sustainability campaign, a month-long programme asking every employee to take one practical step to reduce water and energy use at work. Teams across production, administration, and logistics will follow a shared challenge, with results published at the end of the month.

“Sustainability only works when it is part of the daily routine, not a policy on a wall,” said a Company spokesperson. The campaign supports the Company’s commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, in particular Goal 12, Responsible Consumption and Production.
